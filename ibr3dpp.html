Privacy Policy for Indian Bikes Riding 3D

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Skorax (“we”, “us”, “our”), the developer of the mobile game Indian Bikes Riding 3D (the “Game”), handles information when you play the Game on iPhone or iPad. By using the Game you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

At a glance

  • The Game has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise (GPS) location.
  • The Game is free and shows ads. Our advertising, analytics and attribution partners automatically collect some device and usage information, described below.
  • On iOS we ask your permission before your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for tracking. You can change this at any time in your device settings.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

1. Information we collect

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, precise GPS location, or payment information. The Game does not offer in-app purchases or user accounts.

Information collected automatically by our partners

The third-party software development kits (SDKs) built into the Game may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers – the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV), and identifiers generated by the SDKs.
  • IP address and approximate location – a general location (such as country or city) derived from your IP address. We do not use precise GPS location.
  • Device and network information – device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, network type and mobile carrier.
  • Usage data – when and how long you play, game events and features used, and app version.
  • Advertising data – which ads were shown, ad clicks and interactions, and ad revenue information.
  • Diagnostics – crash logs and performance data.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, unlocked items and settings are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

2. How the information is used

  • To show ads in the Game, including personalized ads where you have given permission or consent;
  • To measure ad performance, limit how often ads are shown, and attribute app installs to advertising campaigns;
  • To understand how players use the Game so we can fix problems and improve gameplay;
  • To adjust some game and ad settings remotely (for example, how often ads appear) without releasing an update;
  • To detect and prevent ad fraud and to keep the Game secure;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Tracking and your choices on iOS

When you first open the Game, iOS may ask whether you allow the Game to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency).

  • If you allow tracking, our advertising partners may use your IDFA to show more relevant ads and measure them.
  • If you do not allow tracking, your IDFA is not shared, and you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized advertising is used, through a consent message provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform and/or AppLovin. We and our partners rely on your consent for personalized advertising, and on legitimate interests for non-personalized advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To change your consent choices, you can reinstall the Game, which shows the consent message again, or contact us. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Users in the United States

Under some US state privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), sharing device identifiers with advertising partners for personalized advertising may count as “selling” or “sharing” personal information. You can opt out by not allowing tracking in iOS (see Section 4) or by contacting us. We will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

7. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Data retention

We do not run our own servers that store your personal data. Our partners keep the information they collect for as long as their own privacy policies describe. Analytics data in Google Firebase is kept for a limited period and then deleted automatically.

9. Deleting your data

Because the Game has no accounts, the simplest way to delete the data stored on your device is to uninstall the Game. To ask for data held by our partners to be deleted, email us at the address below. We will help by passing your request to the relevant partners where possible. You can also contact the partners directly using the links in Section 3.

10. Security

We and our partners use reasonable technical measures, such as encrypted (HTTPS) connections, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

Our partners may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, where data protection laws may differ. Where required, they use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date above will change. Please check this page from time to time.
