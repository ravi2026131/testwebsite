Privacy Policy for Ultimate Car Racing Simulator

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Ultimate Car Racing Simulator (the "Game", "App" or "Service"), developed by Divyansh Gangwar ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you download, install and play it.

By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Summary

  • The Game does not require an account, login or registration.
  • We do not ask for, and do not directly collect, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise (GPS) location.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The Game shows advertisements and uses third-party services (listed below) that may automatically collect certain device and usage information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

None. The Game has no sign-up, chat or form, so we do not collect any personal information you type in. If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message, and we use it only to reply to you.

2.2 Game data stored on your device

Game progress, unlocked cars, coins, scores and settings (such as sound, controls and graphics quality) are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game.

2.3 Information collected automatically by third-party services

The third-party advertising and service providers used in the Game may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers: such as the Android Advertising ID or similar identifiers.
  • Approximate location: derived from your device's IP address (not precise GPS location).
  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, language, screen size and network type.
  • App interactions: how you interact with ads and the Game, such as ads viewed or clicked and session length.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data.

This information is collected and processed by those third parties under their own privacy policies.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To run the Game and keep it working correctly.
  • To show advertisements, including rewarded ads, which keep the Game free.
  • To measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud.
  • To understand crashes and improve the Game's performance.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for direct marketing by email, SMS or phone.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services. Each one has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read:

  • Google Play Services: Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Unity Ads: Privacy Policy

We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these third parties.

5. Advertising Choices and Consent

Some ads may be personalized based on your device's advertising identifier. Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.

You can limit personalized ads at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads), then choose Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, then turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track.

You will still see ads after opting out, but they will be less tailored to you.

6. Permissions

The Game only uses the permissions needed to work and show ads, such as internet access and network state. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us at the email below and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store your personal information on our own servers. Game data on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game.

Data collected by third-party services is kept according to their own retention policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID as described in Section 5. If you would like help with a deletion request to any of our partners, email us and we will assist you.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information related to the Game. However, no method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Data Transfers

Our third-party partners may process information on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. These partners apply their own safeguards for such transfers.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to raise a grievance. Because we do not hold personal data about you directly, most requests are handled through your device settings or the third-party services listed above. You can also contact us and we will help.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the Game after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
